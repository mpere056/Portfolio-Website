# Museum Dynamic Creative Review

Date: 2026-07-20
Reading time: about two minutes
Status: accepted as `1A, 2A, 3A` on 2026-07-20
Purpose: retained decision record for the completed `ART-12C` gate

## The Proposed Result

The existing static Museum image will **not** remain the normal visual experience with effects placed over it. It is retained only as a loading frame, reduced-motion composition, and emergency fallback.

The normal Projects page will be reconstructed as a layered dynamic world:

- Background gas, cloud, fog, and distant light evolve continuously at different depths.
- Coral fans and tendrils remain rooted while separate bodies and tips sway independently.
- Mechanical rings, lenses, apertures, and contraptions rotate around credible pivots at different rates.
- Cyan, amber, and coral streams visibly flow in one direction, change width and brightness, and carry semantic connections through the world.
- Buildings and large instruments stay architecturally stable, but their windows, internal light, reflections, shadows, surrounding fog, and passing matter change.
- Water and dark ground receive delayed reflections, moving caustics, and low mist.
- Sparse translucent organisms, spores, fragments, and shadows sometimes pass in front of or behind other objects.
- Pointer, focus, project selection, and knowledge-graph relationships redirect or intensify motion already happening; motion does not begin only when the visitor interacts.

At normal stimulation, every dominant region participates. Nothing should read as an untouched background poster.

## First Production Proof

The recommended first proof uses the lower-left Museum ecology: coral, the cyan organism-instrument, lower streams, fog, reflective ground, and near foreground matter.

This is **not** the final area that will move. It is the smallest production test that forces us to solve most of the difficult material types together. If it works, the same reviewed production process expands across the central book, orbital core, east observatory, buildings, mechanisms, upper streams, and distant atmosphere until the whole page has no dominant static dead zone.

The first proof will contain independently movable transparent layers and control maps, not a new flattened image.

## Motion Character

Recommended character: **quietly alive at idle, more expressive around attention**.

- Idle movement is continuous, slow, layered, and materially believable.
- Different materials use different timing; the page never pulses or drifts as one object.
- Interaction bends, gathers, aligns, illuminates, or clarifies existing motion.
- Foreground interruptions are rare enough to preserve elegance and usability.
- Text and controls remain stable and readable.
- Reduced motion freezes into an intentionally composed still world.

## Explicitly Not Building

- A static background with more particles on top.
- One image that slowly pans, zooms, breathes, or warps.
- Rectangular parallax slices cut from the current image.
- Every object moving in the same rhythm.
- Constant high-energy motion competing with project labels.
- Bulk asset generation for the entire Museum before one material stack proves the approach.

## Three Decisions

### 1. Dynamic World Direction

- **A - Approve (recommended):** the static image is fallback-only; the normal scene is rebuilt from independently moving material layers.
- **B - Revise:** keep the layered model but change a behavior described above.

### 2. First Proof Area

- **A - Lower-left ecology (recommended):** proves coral, organism, flow, fog, reflection, light, and foreground depth together.
- **B - Central book and orbital core:** proves architecture, page/light behavior, spheres, cage mechanics, and streams first.
- **C - East observatory:** proves the largest mechanisms, architectural lighting, lattice depth, and stream occlusion first.

All three areas will eventually be dynamic. This choice only determines where production starts.

### 3. Motion Intensity

- **A - Quiet and deep (recommended):** nearly everything changes, but most motion is subtle until attention approaches.
- **B - Clearly animated:** motion is easier to notice immediately while remaining elegant.
- **C - Dramatic and surreal:** stronger deformation, faster flow, more foreground passage, and more visible light transformation.

## Reply Format

Reply with the three letters and any correction, for example:

`1A, 2A, 3A. Begin the first dynamic material proof.`

That approval authorizes `ART-12D` asset production for the selected proof area. It does not approve a static background or declare the rest of the Museum complete.
