## Purpose

These briefs use the current production content and interactions. They are not speculative redesigns that avoid the hard parts of the real page. Each frame must prove that the selected direction can replace the current card-grid and nested-panel aesthetic without losing product truth, navigation, accessibility, or the depth grammar.

These are visual-production inputs for `ART-01`. A selected output becomes implementation-ready only when its aesthetic art packet is completed using `19-Aesthetic-System-Integration-And-Delivery.md`; runtime primitives and production remediation belong to `ART-02` and `ART-03`.

The first visual round should include three intentionally different compositions for Brief 1 and at least one strong composition for Briefs 2 and 3. Preserve one rejected museum alternative with a written reason.

## Brief 1: Museum Lobby / Signal

### Emotional Thesis

The visitor has entered a dark observatory where nine bodies are not yet fully classified. Some are instruments, some specimens, some disturbances, and some historical traces. Moving closer changes what can be perceived.

### Composition

- Full desktop field with charged black negative space rather than a centered content column.
- Three flagship phenomena form the strongest spatial anchors but do not line up as equal cards.
- LifeInbox appears as a dark receiving shell containing one warm, stable point and faint outer membranes.
- Dreamlife appears as a translucent lens or suspended pearl casting three incomplete spectral paths.
- Sudoku Together appears as a pale lattice or resonant grid fragment with one visitor pulse and one quieter answering pulse.
- Historical projects form two or three curated clusters: a painted/glitched Discord trace, a mathematical specimen, and an early-game/mod archive ecology.
- Fine knowledge-graph lines become visible only around the nearest or selected phenomenon.

### Typography

- “Museum of working systems” becomes a quiet orientation mark, not a repeated eyebrow style.
- Project names appear as annotations revealed near phenomena, following their geometry where appropriate.
- One concise invitation remains legible without requiring hover.
- Year, lifecycle, and technical metadata appear only after Approach.

### Interaction State

- Default: silhouettes and a few internal emissions establish distinct regions.
- Proximity/focus: one phenomenon gains material detail and one concise label; related bodies answer faintly.
- Selection: the field recomposes around the chosen project instead of only scrolling to a standard panel.
- Return: the selected phenomenon settles back into its place while preserving discovery state.

### Utility Furniture

- Navigation, Quick Tour, archive AI, and stimulation controls share one quiet observatory rail or edge logic.
- The rail must remain discoverable but cannot form four unrelated floating pills.
- Sound invitation appears only when contextually earned and cannot cover project content.

### Calm State

- No idle camera drift is required.
- Phenomena remain clearly composed with static translucency, linework, and contrast.
- Semantic relationships can use line weight and value rather than animation.

### Rejection Conditions

- Three-column or masonry card grid remains visible.
- Every signal uses the same bounding shape.
- Identity depends on title and accent color.
- The result resembles a space-themed dashboard or generic WebGL portfolio.
- Spectral color is spread evenly rather than concentrated in meaningful phenomena.

## Brief 2: LifeInbox / Handle

### Emotional Thesis

A chaotic thought becomes trustworthy the instant it crosses a local boundary. Intelligence comes later. The scene should make “saved now” physically and emotionally distinct from “interpreted later.”

### Composition

- One unstable thread of words, pigment, or luminous residue enters from open darkness.
- A receiving instrument occupies the focal area: translucent but not a glass card, with a dark outer silhouette and a warm interior boundary.
- The editable thought is part of the phenomenon. Accessible text input remains real HTML but visually reads as a line or residue being held, not a textarea box.
- The capture action is attached to the receiving boundary, not presented as a generic filled pill beneath a form.
- Explanatory copy sits outside the focal mechanism as restrained annotation.

### Capture Transition

1. The thought is visually unstable but legible.
2. Activating capture causes it to condense immediately into a stable pearl, seed, or dense paper-like specimen.
3. A local identifier and timestamp appear as fine notation anchored to the captured object.
4. Outer sync and enrichment membranes remain dormant, proving that local trust did not wait for them.

### Later Organization

- “Reveal organization” opens a second translucent boundary outside the stable core.
- The illustrative reminder appears as a possible orbit, label, or attached fragment, never replacing the visibly stored original.
- Verified and illustrative states differ by material and line language, not green success-card versus gray card.

### Calm And Accessible State

- Text contrast remains strong.
- Input, capture, reset, and next-depth controls remain keyboard reachable and semantically named.
- Reduced motion uses a direct state change from unstable trace to settled specimen.

### Rejection Conditions

- Standard textarea, primary button, and two result cards remain the perceived composition.
- Green is used as a generic success shorthand.
- Local, sync, and AI stages look like tabs or a progress stepper.
- The biological quality becomes wet, threatening, or unrelated to data trust.

## Brief 3: LifeInbox / Understand

### Emotional Thesis

The stable captured thought is opened without being destroyed. The visitor sees several promises nested around it and can inspect where each promise begins, what it can prove, and what remains illustrative.

### Composition

- The captured specimen remains central, preserving continuity from Handle.
- Four boundaries unfold around it as asymmetrical translucent membranes, rings, folds, or shells: local capture, private sync, illustrative enrichment, useful return.
- Boundaries have distinct material behavior rather than four equal architecture cards.
- Fine waveforms and diagram lines show crossings between layers.
- The selected boundary comes into focus while others remain structurally visible.

### Evidence Integration

- Repository evidence, the trust field note, and living project state attach as score-like annotations or specimen labels.
- Each source visibly points to the claim it supports.
- Planned or illustrative behavior uses a different line/material treatment from verified behavior.
- The six-figure Dreamlife story never appears here; project identities remain accurate.

### Interaction

- Selecting a layer changes focus, scale, and annotation density rather than filling a card below it.
- The deepest state can reveal the whole boundary system at once, then let the visitor isolate one layer.
- The canonical LifeInbox world appears as an opening through or beyond the mechanism, not a generic external-link button.

### Calm And Accessible State

- A static exploded composition retains all relationships.
- Keyboard selection maps clearly to the four boundaries.
- A concise textual reading order exists without duplicating every visual detail.

### Rejection Conditions

- Four equal rounded cards remain the architecture representation.
- A selected state is communicated only by brighter border and background.
- Evidence is placed in a generic footer link row.
- Understand feels like more page content rather than a perceptual deepening.
