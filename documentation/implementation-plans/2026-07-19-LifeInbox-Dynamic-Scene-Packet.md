# LifeInbox Dynamic Scene Packet

Last updated: 2026-07-19

Status: implemented candidate under `ART-08`; local verification passes, Production and Mark acceptance remain.

## Scene Thesis

LifeInbox is a receiving instrument, not an abstract particle effect. An untrusted thought begins diffuse outside the local boundary. Capture condenses it into a dependable local specimen. Organization opens a visibly separate outer membrane. Enter explodes four promises without breaking their connection to the original. Inspecting resurface sends a restrained reminder trace back toward the visitor. Understand turns repository and field-note evidence into part of the instrument rather than detached links.

## Stable Checksum

- Keep the approved LifeInbox vessel artwork as the stable matte and failure frame.
- Keep the existing synthetic capture reducer and explicit statement that no personal data is sent.
- Preserve Handle, Enter, Understand, direct-route, project-world, repository, field-note, and museum-return behavior.

## Renderer Decision

Use DOM/CSS, SVG, and one bounded Canvas 2D field. WebGL is not selected because this product transformation needs state clarity and precise fallback more than fluid refraction. Shared infrastructure stops at pure frame/path functions and lifecycle inputs; LifeInbox owns its targets, materials, and choreography.

## Calm And Failure Contract

- Reduced motion or hidden state sets particle count and continuous energy to zero.
- Canvas failure leaves the matte, ingress/core/membrane state, SVG boundaries, labels, controls, and evidence intact.
- Missing art leaves the route background and semantic instrument intact.
- No state transition depends on animation completion.
- Mobile may simplify or hide Canvas while retaining the independent product interaction.
