# Visual Reference Coverage And Route Art Direction

Date: 2026-07-18
Status: Active correction after Production review
Direction: Impossible Observatory of Living Instruments
Packages: `ART-01`, `ART-03`, `ART-04`, `ART-05`, `ART-06`, renewed `QA-02`

## Decision

The deployed Phase 4 release improved composition and project distinction, but it did not visibly carry enough of the supplied references' artistic intensity. It translated the references mainly into dark space, accent color, simple translucency, glow, and geometric linework. Those are supporting qualities, not the complete visual language.

The next aesthetic pass must visibly include the full range of reference qualities across the portfolio:

- Bioluminescent ecology and unfamiliar living terrain.
- Internally illuminated familiar artifacts.
- Nacre, liquid glass, pearls, ribbons, mineral veining, and impossible fluid behavior.
- Organic apertures, eyes, spirals, shells, and radial repetition.
- Alchemical vessels, bubbles, frost, vapor, and precious theatrical matter.
- Painterly shadow, scraped pigment, and human imperfection.
- Intricate organism-like diagrams and controlled white space.
- Spectral waveform, glitch, smoke, and architecture-creature hybrids.

The supplied screenshots remain taste references only. They are not production assets and should not be reproduced composition-for-composition. Original scenes, forms, textures, and motion must be created for Mark's content.

## Why The Current Translation Fell Short

The current implementation contains useful structural work, but it is visually weighted toward the easiest code-native layers:

| Visual layer | Current strength | Current gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silhouette and layout | Better than the original card grid | Forms are still too abstractly simple and UI-adjacent |
| Light and color | Spectral accents and local glow exist | Light rarely appears to live inside complex matter |
| Diagram | Fine lines and project-specific geometry exist | Diagrams lack organism-level intricacy and material integration |
| Surface | Basic gradients, borders, blur, and translucency exist | Little pigment, nacre, resin, frost, paper fiber, vapor, or mineral texture |
| Artifact | Project metaphors are named | Few scenes contain a memorable made object with an impossible interior |
| Depth | More content and project-specific states exist | Material physics and perceptual scale do not transform enough |
| Human trace | Copy is personal and factual | Painterly, handwritten, scored, revised, or handled qualities are rare |

The correction is therefore not a CSS polish pass. It requires original visual production, compositing, and scene direction before more component styling.

## Art Density By Surface

Artistic richness should be distributed intentionally rather than flattened for consistency.

### Hero-Density Scenes

Home threshold, Museum Signal, and one deepest scene for each flagship must be able to stand as compelling artwork without the navigation or explanatory copy. They need strong silhouette, complex original matter, local detail, controlled light, and a meaningful interaction.

### Exhibit-Density Scenes

Approach, Handle, and most historical-project views retain one major artifact plus quieter annotation. They should feel authored but leave enough perceptual room for interaction and truth.

### Reading-Density Scenes

About prose, blogs, evidence, and utility states prioritize reading. Their artistry appears through margins, page structure, localized diagrams, material transitions, and occasional focal artifacts rather than continuous spectacle.

Lower density does not mean default cards, plain gradients, or uncomposed empty space.

## Required Scene Stack

Each hero-density scene must deliberately address all seven layers. A layer may be static or subtle, but it cannot be omitted accidentally.

1. **Distant silhouette:** one recognizable field or artifact before labels.
2. **Authored form:** an original organism, vessel, book, lattice, aperture, or disturbance.
3. **Surface matter:** pigment, paper, resin, nacre, frost, mineral, vapor, porcelain, or graphite with visible local behavior.
4. **Internal light:** a semantically located emitter, not a glow behind a container.
5. **Diagram or notation:** technical truth integrated into the object through score, anatomy, waveform, ledger, or evidence marks.
6. **Legible interaction:** real semantic HTML controls spatially attached to the scene rather than floating above it.
7. **Depth transformation:** Handle, Enter, or Understand changes matter, scale, anatomy, or spatial rules rather than appending another panel.

The current release is strongest in layers 1, 4, and 5. The next pass must prioritize original authored form, surface matter, and depth transformation.

## Original Asset And Runtime Strategy

High artistic quality will require more than CSS, but rich assets must remain original, maintainable, performant, and semantically subordinate.

### Original Raster Plates And Textures

Use original generated or manually composed raster material for painterly fields, alien ecology, mineral surfaces, vapor, frost, nacre, and complex macro detail. Create the asset for the route's exact composition; do not use the supplied reference screenshot or a lightly edited derivative.

Requirements:

- Preserve prompt/source and usage-rights notes in an asset manifest.
- Export responsive AVIF/WebP sizes and a stable poster frame.
- Separate focal matter, atmosphere, and masks where parallax or reveal needs independent control.
- Avoid embedding essential text or project facts in raster imagery.

### SVG And Code-Native Drawing

Use SVG for score notation, anatomy, waveforms, graph-backed sight lines, apertures, ledger marks, focus geometry, and accessibility-preserving vector states.

SVG should add precision to rich matter rather than carry the entire scene alone.

### Canvas, WebGL, And Shaders

Use selectively for liquid refraction, responsive waveform distortion, living-grid behavior, depth-based masking, and local material transitions. These effects need a static poster and reduced-motion path with the same focal hierarchy.

Do not make every route depend on a continuously running canvas or one universal shader.

### Semantic HTML And CSS

HTML owns copy, inputs, buttons, focus order, links, evidence, and recovery. CSS owns composition, layering, typography, blend, masks, and capability-aware fallbacks. Controls should appear attached to artifacts while remaining real controls.

### Three-Dimensional Assets

Use only when volume materially improves the idea, such as the Dreamlife pearl, LifeInbox vessel, or an inspectable artifact. A strong 2D/2.5D composition is preferable to generic 3D geometry.

## Depth Translation

| Stage | Visual requirement under the corrected direction |
| --- | --- |
| Signal | A distant silhouette or disturbance identifies the project family without title or accent color |
| Approach | Surface matter and internal structure become visible; one tension and action resolve |
| Handle | The visitor directly changes the artifact's material or logical state |
| Enter | The artifact becomes an environment, anatomy, or altered scale rather than a larger interface |

The same project artifact should persist through depth whenever continuity helps understanding. It may open, shed, crystallize, refract, uncoil, or become anatomical; it should not disappear and be replaced by a standard explanation panel.

## Creative Review Gates

The next candidate cannot pass from route correctness, distinct accent colors, or a general statement that it is more artistic.

### Reference Coverage Gate

- Every `R1` through `R9` quality has a named primary route and an implemented or explicitly pending keyframe.
- A stable capture demonstrates the quality without copying the supplied composition.
- No reference family is represented only by a color token or generic glow.

### Artwork Gate

- Home, Museum, and each flagship deepest scene pass the crop and stillness tests as compositions.
- At least one close crop per hero scene reveals meaningful local material detail.
- Removing copy and utility controls leaves a deliberate artistic image.

### Material Physics Gate

- Reviewers can identify what emits, transmits, reflects, absorbs, condenses, fractures, records, or decays.
- Material changes have semantic causes tied to project behavior or depth.
- Iridescence, vapor, eyes, spirals, and organisms are not decorative filler.

### Cohesion Gate

- Routes feel made by one artist because they share perceptual laws, annotation discipline, and causal depth.
- Routes do not feel templated because their artifact, surface, composition, and transformation differ.
- Utility controls and reading text remain calm enough that the artwork retains hierarchy.

### Mark Review Gate

Mark reviews the selected keyframes before broad runtime implementation and reviews stable Production or Preview captures before creative acceptance. Feedback names accepted visual families and any route whose artistic intensity remains below the references.
