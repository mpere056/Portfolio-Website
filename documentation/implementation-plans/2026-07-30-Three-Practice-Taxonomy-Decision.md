# Three-Practice Taxonomy Decision

Date: 2026-07-30  
Status: Confirmed  
Supersedes: the four-practice taxonomy in the 2026-07-24 and 2026-07-26 Home decisions

## Decision

Home and project navigation use three distinct practices:

1. **Music & Performance**: performances, arrangements, teaching, music resources, and musical tools.
2. **Life Systems & Tools**: Dreamlife, LifeInbox, practical software, personal systems, and tools for clearer everyday work.
3. **Play & Community**: Sudoku Together, Discord/community projects, games, mods, and the Interactive Story Generator.

AI is not a fourth destination. It is a cross-cutting capability and material that can appear inside any practice, in project evidence, in the knowledge graph, and through the site-wide archive AI.

## Consequences

- Home presents three practice instruments around the piano.
- Dreamlife moves to `life-systems-tools`.
- Interactive Story Generator moves to `play-community`.
- `practice:ai-possible-futures` and `territory:ai-futures` are removed before public practice routes ship.
- The accepted observatory proof remains reusable visual material for AI-heavy projects and transitions; it no longer defines a navigation category.
- Checkpoint `G` now means completing all three practice environments after the first preview/selected packet, not restoring a fourth category.

## Rationale

The three practices describe recognizable kinds of work and visitor intent. AI describes how Mark builds across those practices, so making it a peer category produced overlap rather than a clear destination.
