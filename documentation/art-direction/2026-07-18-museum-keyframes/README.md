# Museum Keyframe Decision

These keyframes translate the approved material studies into three possible Museum compositions. They are decision evidence, not production backgrounds to be copied without interaction.

## Selected: 01 Phenomenon-led ecology

This direction best supports exploration. Projects exist as distinct phenomena in one impossible nocturnal landscape rather than as cards or objects arranged on a shelf. It supplies nine memorable landmarks, preserves darkness between discoveries, and gives hover, focus, movement, and semantic lighting room to operate.

Production use:

- The landscape is a visual substrate beneath real HTML project signals.
- Every landmark has a stable project coordinate and an accessible text label.
- Focus and hover classify a phenomenon; selection opens the existing Approach, Handle, Enter, and Understand depth system.
- The artwork is darkened and cropped responsively rather than treated as a static poster.

## Reassigned: 02 Artifact-led observatory

This direction is visually strong but reads as a curated cabinet of objects. That weakens the Museum's discovery physics, so it is not the lobby composition. Its translucent instrument language is reassigned to the About timeline and quiet AI archive, where an inspectable knowledge artifact is appropriate.

## Reassigned: 03 Painterly diagrammatic field

This direction has the best sense of authorship and emergence, but it offers fewer stable project landmarks. Its painterly presence language is reassigned to Home and historical About moments, where an identity can emerge without needing nine precise navigation targets.

## Runtime constraints

- The selected art never replaces semantic navigation, focus order, text, or project-state truth.
- Reduced-motion mode retains lighting and depth without autonomous movement.
- Small screens use an independently composed signal list over a calmer crop.
- Route-specific materials stay related through darkness, translucency, refraction, fine evidence lines, and selective warm signal.
