## Runtime Composition

The observatory uses one compact crop as its stable/failure checksum. Normal motion is code-generated: three sampled optical annuli rotate at unequal ratios, the central lens refracts, cyan and gold packets travel through a procedural current, two haze depths advect independently, diagram rings and diffraction respond near attention, and sparse Three.js particles traverse the scene. The accepted production Museum membrane, aperture, mesh, halo, veil, and particle response are reused exactly above the route-local compositor.

The structure itself does not wobble. Reduced motion removes WebGL and preserves the crop. Hidden tabs stop the render loop. The proof remains one viewport and is isolated from `/projects` and `/projects/ambient-proof`.
