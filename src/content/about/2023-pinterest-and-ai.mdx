I had never tried growing a social media account before, so I wanted to try.

This became a good way for me to feel motivated to experiment with and create with generative AI.

So I looked up trends and iterated through designs with Midjourney and StableDiffusion with ComfyUI.

After about a month, I started seeing some traction.

Within five months, I was getting 1 million monthly views across my posts.
