Dreamlife started from a simple observation: most planning tools are good at storing goals, but weaker at helping someone understand why a goal matters.

The project is built around a loop of visioning, exploration, and refinement. The app helps a person name what they want, test different versions of that future, and convert the signal into smaller experiments.

The AI layer is meant to support judgment rather than replace it. It extracts themes, asks better questions, and helps turn raw reflection into a direction that feels usable.
