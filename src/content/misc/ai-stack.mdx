---

The chatbot loads content from my MDX files, converts it to plain text, chunks it, and stores canonical content records with 768-dimensional vector embeddings in Cloud Firestore.

At query time, it embeds your question, runs Firestore nearest-neighbour search, selects a small relevant subset, and sends that context to Gemini Flash. The database remains private to server credentials, while the site streams the grounded answer to the visitor.
