My personal productivity system uses Todoist, Notion, Google Calendar, and Google Gemini, all working together, multiple APIs involved, and managed by a Hetzner VPS.
It starts on Todoist.
I use Todoist just for input, because it's so light and frictionless to open up on my phone and use to enter items into.
I created 6 labels in Todoist - Journal, Project, Task, Notes, Calendar, and Copied.
Whenever I create a task in Todoist, I assign it a label.
My VPS periodically checks my Todoist for new tasks to determine how the task should be organized, based on the label I gave it, and if it finds a task that doesn't have the label Copied assigned to it then it assigns that task the label Copied.
My VPS removes copied tasks are from Todoist after about 24h.
Now based on the label of the task, my VPS will either sort it somewhere into Notion, or if it's a Calendar event, it'll use AI to extract info like content, date, duration, and if it's a recurring or not, then it'll appropriately add it into my Google Calendar.
