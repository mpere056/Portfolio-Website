LifeInbox is a local-first personal organizer built around fast capture, reliable sync, and AI-assisted organization.

Instead of being a coaching or life-design product, it focuses on making everyday inputs useful: tasks, reminders, shopping notes, journal fragments, people, projects, and private notes all land in one trusted Android-first system.
